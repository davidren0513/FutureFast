
## AndroidLiquidGlass catalog interactions

The main-screen liquid navigation adapts LiquidToggle, LiquidButton, LiquidBottomTabs, LiquidBottomTab, DampedDragAnimation, DragGestureInspector and InteractiveHighlight from [Kyant0/AndroidLiquidGlass](https://github.com/Kyant0/AndroidLiquidGlass/tree/65ab177e90e5c1d8c62e70cf7755841982da65f6). Copyright Kyant0 and contributors; Apache License 2.0. Changes: native Android frame/clock integration, app theme and accessibility selection state, clamped tab selection, equivalent capsule shapes supported by the pinned dependency, and disabled-button handling. GlassSwitch adapts LiquidToggle’s sampled track and spring lens using Android native toggleable/draggable semantics. The existing Backdrop rendering dependency remains pinned.
